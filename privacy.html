Privacy Policy

Effective [DATE]. Operated by [YOUR LEGAL BUSINESS NAME], [BUSINESS ADDRESS].

Post reminders

If you turn on reminders, your device's notification address and the names and times of your scheduled posts are stored on our server, which runs on Cloudflare, so we can notify you at post time. Turning reminders off deletes them from our server. Reminders are deleted a week after they are sent.

What stays on your device

Projects, photos you add, brand details, notifications and activity counts are stored in your browser or app storage on your device. We do not receive them unless a feature below sends them.

What is sent to our servers

If AI writing is on, the description you type, your brand name, the ad type and any website details you keep selected are sent to our server and to our AI provider, Anthropic, to write ad copy. If you use From Website, the address you enter is fetched by our server. If a site cannot be read directly, its domain may be sent to Anthropic to search that site. If you create an AI image or clip, your description, and any scene image you animate, is sent to Runway. If you report a result, the report and the ad text are sent to us. Standard web logs such as IP address may be processed for security and rate limiting.

Website kit

When you enter a website, our server reads its public home page, up to three related pages, and their images so you can use them in an ad. Images are saved on your device only if you choose to generate with them.

AI voiceover

If you choose an AI voice, your video script is sent to our server and voice provider, ElevenLabs, to create the audio. AI voices are labeled as AI-generated in the video.

Microphone

Voiceover uses your microphone only while you record. Recordings stay on your device unless you export or share a video.

Advertising

The free web version may show ads from Google AdSense or similar partners, which may use cookies or device identifiers. [Add your ad partner and consent details before enabling ads.] Premium does not show ads.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play or our checkout provider. We do not receive your full payment card number.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. Your photos, clips and voice recordings are not uploaded by the features in this version.

Sharing

When you share an ad, your device sends it to the app you choose. That app's own policy applies.

Your choices

You can export or delete your data in Profile. See how to delete your data.

Children

Creatagious is intended for business users and is not directed to children under 13.
